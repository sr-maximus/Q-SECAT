# Glossary

**CBOM** — Cryptography Bill of Materials; representation of cryptographic assets and dependencies.

**CRQC** — Cryptographically Relevant Quantum Computer; a quantum computer capable of materially breaking cryptography in scope.

**Crypto agility** — Ability to replace or adapt cryptographic algorithms, protocols, keys, hardware and dependencies while preserving security and operations.

**Hybrid workflow** — Iterative workflow in which classical compute and quantum resources exchange parameters, jobs, measurements or optimization state.

**PQC** — Post-Quantum Cryptography; cryptographic algorithms executed on classical systems and intended to resist classical and quantum attacks.

**QAL** — Q-SECAT Assurance Level.

**QKD** — Quantum Key Distribution; use of quantum states/properties to establish keying material, with security dependent on protocol and implementation assumptions.

**QPU** — Quantum Processing Unit.

**QRNG** — Quantum Random Number Generator; RNG using a quantum physical process as an entropy source.

**QSD/QTA/QTE/QSC/QSTEST/QST** — Q-SECAT domain, tactic, technique, control, test and Top-10 identifiers.

**Quantum network** — Network that distributes quantum states or entanglement and uses classical control/support channels.

**Quantum sensing/metrology** — Measurement technologies exploiting quantum effects for sensitivity, precision, timing or reference capabilities.

**Store-now-decrypt-later** — Collection of currently encrypted data for attempted decryption when future capabilities become available.

**Threat horizon** — Scenario assumption for when a relevant adversarial capability may exist; not a certain forecast.
